to delivering world-class medical services through advanced technology, skilled specialists, and compassionate patient care. Strategically located in Mohali, the hospital serves patients from Punjab, Chandigarh, and neighboring regions with comprehensive healthcare solutions designed to meet modern medical needs. The hospital offers specialized treatment across departments including Cardiology, Oncology, Orthopaedics, Neurosciences, Gastroenterology, Internal Medicine, Critical Care, Paediatrics, and Women & Child Care. Supported by experienced doctors and trained healthcare professionals, Park Hospital Mohali focuses on delivering accurate diagnosis, evidence-based treatment, and personalized patient care. Equipped with modern operation theatres, advanced intensive care units, diagnostic laboratories, and imaging technology, the hospital ensures seamless medical management for both routine and complex health conditions. The emergency and trauma care services are operational round the clock to provide timely medical intervention during emergencies. Park Hospital Mohali follows a patient-first approach where clinical excellence is balanced with empathy, comfort, and transparency. The hospital's multidisciplinary healthcare model encourages collaborative treatment planning among specialists, ensuring better clinical outcomes and comprehensive patient management. In addition to curative treatments, the hospital actively promotes preventive healthcare through wellness programs, health camps, and routine screenings aimed at encouraging healthier communities. The facility also emphasizes patient education and awareness to help individuals make informed healthcare decisions. With modern infrastructure, comfortable patient facilities, and a commitment to ethical healthcare practices, Park Hospital Mohali continues to build trust among patients and families.

About Dr. Manuj Wadhwa With a remarkable track record of over 40,000 joint replacement surgeries, Dr. Manuj Wadhwa has earned recognition for performing the highest number of such procedures. His exceptional achievements have been duly acknowledged by the Limca Book of World Records and underscored by seven published records. Dr. Wadhwa's illustrious career includes being honored with the prestigious Ranawat Adult Joint Reconstruction Fellowship at Lenox Hill Hospital in New York, USA, where he trained under the tutelage of Dr. C. S. Ranawat, renowned for operating on former Prime Minister Mr. A. B. Vajpayee. Dr. Wadhwa's pursuit of excellence has taken him across the globe for super-specialty training in joint replacement surgery, encompassing countries such as the USA, U.K., Australia, Singapore, France, Germany, and South Korea. Additionally, he has honed his skills at eminent institutions like AIIMS Delhi, Hinduja Hospital Mumbai, and various other leading hospitals in India and abroad. Among his many pioneering contributions to orthopedic surgery, Dr. Wadhwa holds the distinction of launching the First Digital Ortho Suite in the Asia Pacific region, a landmark event inaugurated by the Honorable Chief Minister of Punjab. He has introduced groundbreaking concepts such as the "30 years of knee concept" and "Personal fit joint replacement" in India, along with spearheading the introduction of the high-flexion knee and hip replacements, computer and CT-based surgeries, as well as advancements in shoulder, elbow, and finger joint replacements in North India. Internationally recognized as a designer surgeon for knee and hip joint implants, Dr. Wadhwa continues to push the boundaries of innovation in orthopedic care, enriching the lives of countless patients through his expertise and dedication.
